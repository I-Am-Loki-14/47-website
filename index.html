About Us

Hello, welcome to 47 F&B Group

Our 47 group is a locally-owned and operated chain of restaurants dedicated to pro­viding diverse culinary experiences to our customers. Based in Yangon, Myanmar, we're known for our diverse, delicious and the richness of global cuisines while offering a welcoming and vibrant dining atmosphere. Our story began in 2014. "47" is named after its inaugural branch, situated at the intersection of 47th Street and Anawyahtar Road.

Our Branches

Thai 47 Downtown

Downtown Branch Established in 2014, Thai 47 Downtown marks the beginning of the 47 Group's journey, setting the stage for a legacy of exceptional dining experiences. Located in a prime downtown area, this flagship outlet serves a bustling crowd with a menu featuring light Thai cuisine, aromatic Thai tea, and rich coffee. Thai 47 Downtown is not just a restaurant; it's where our story began, inviting customers to be a part of our continuing adventure. The restaurant has earned the Tripadvisor Certificate of Excellence award in 2017 and 2018 consecutively, recognizing its outstanding service and quality

Thai 47 KyaukKone

Kyauk Kone Branch Launched in 2015 in response to increasing customer demand, Thai 47 Kyauk Kone quickly became a leading presence on the once-quiet Kyauk Kone Road. The restaurant features expansive dining spaces, including elegantly appointed indoor, outdoor, and private areas, all designed to enhance the dining experience. We take pride in offering authentic Thai cuisine and exemplary customer service, establishing ourselves as a cornerstone of the local food scene. Our commitment to excellence was recognized in both 2023 and 2024 when we received the prestigious foodpanda Best Performing Restaurant Award and a Certificate of Excellence, further affirming our status as a favorite destination for casual diners.

Thai 47 Helpin

Helpin Branch Guided by the owner's vision, Thai 47 Helpin opened its doors in 2017 near the Royal-Thai Embassy and other prestigious diplomatic posts. This upscale establishment serves authentic Thai cuisine in an elegant setting, where the decor seamlessly blends Burmese and Thai cultural elements. The ambiance is perfect for both intimate celebrations and corporate events. Popular dishes, such as the Grilled Pork Neck, Tom Yam Kung, and Traditional Seabass Tamarind Sauce, consistently receive acclaim for their authentic flavors and high-quality preparation. At Thai 47 Helpin, every detail reflects a commitment to excellence and a passion for delivering memorable dining experiences.

Chinese 47

Chinese 47 was born out of a passion for innovation in 2018 and is located in the heart of Yangon. As the biggest, busiest, and most spacious among the 47 Group's outlets, it offers classic Chinese dishes with a modern twist from across China. Famous for its Roasted Peking Duck and premium Dim Sum, Chinese 47 is also a top-rated venue for breakfast, customer engagement parties, company event dinners, and birthday parties. Renowned for its exceptional customer service, the restaurant features large private rooms, ensuring comfort and privacy for guests seeking an exclusive dining experience.

Burma 47

Launched within the Chinese 47 compound in 2020, Burma 47 enriches Yangon's breakfast scene by adding authentic Shan and traditional Burmese tea house selections. This outlet quickly became a go-to spot for morning gatherings, offering everything from baby Ei kyar kway to Burmese mohinga, and Shan tofu tote, savored by an increasing number of patrons. In recognition of its outstanding quality, Burma 47 received the Certificate of Excellence Award from foodpanda in 2024.

Pinlon House

Pinlon House, opened in 2020 in the burgeoning North Dagon township, offers a fusion of Thai and Asian cuisines, masterfully crafted by our well-experienced chefs whose passion for authentic flavors is evident in every dish. Designed with families in mind, this venue not only caters to culinary treats but also features live football screenings and an unplugged music setting, making it a perfect destination for casual dining and entertainment.

Olive & Twist

Our new authentic menu, elegant decorations, ambient surroundings and heart warming service will ensure your satisfactions.Olive and Twist presents a modern European menu in a setting that’s ideal for both celebratory events and business meetings. It’s a haven for cocktail enthusiasts and wine lovers alike, featuring carefully selected wines from 8 countries with over 44 varieties available. Our handcrafted signature cocktails are sure to be loved by all who try them. Leading the culinary team is a chef with over 25 years of experience specializing in Italian, French, and Mediterranean cuisines. He skillfully blends tradition with innovation to create exquisite dishes that reflect the diverse flavors of Europe. His meticulous focus on high-quality sourced ingredients showcases a mastery of classic and modern culinary techniques, ensuring a premium dining experience for every guest.

Chapter 8

Located in Terminal M Shopping Mall, Mingaladon Township, Chapter 8 opened in 2022 as the ultimate casual dining and nightlife destination. Named for being the eighth restaurant in our group, Chapter 8 features modern decoration and has quickly become a popular beer hangout spot. It offers a range of Thai dishes and bar snacks complemented by an extensive beer and cocktail menu, with both indoor and outdoor seating available. Whether you’re looking to enjoy a lively evening out or a casual meal in a stylish setting, Chapter 8 caters

TORA Modern Izakaya

TORA Modern Izakaya introduces a slice of Japan to Myanmar. Opened in 2023 onInyamyaing Road, it’s become a popular hangout for both locals and expatriates,particularly the Japanese community. This flagship venue of the 47 Group showcases acommitment to authenticity, quality ingredients, and a vibrant izakaya atmosphere.Warayaki (straw-grilled) dishes, particularly aromatic and accompanied by Yakitori, areour signature offerings and have become extremely popular. The venue also features aprivate room with traditional Zashiki seating. Guests can choose from over 60 varietiesof Sake, Shochu, and Japanese Whiskey, making it a premier destination for Japanesecuisine and culture enthusiasts.
