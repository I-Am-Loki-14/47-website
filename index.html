Hello, welcome to 47 F&B Group

Our 47 group is a locally-owned and operated chain of restaurants dedicated to pro­viding diverse culinary experiences to our customers. Based in Yangon, Myanmar, we're known for our diverse, delicious and the richness of global cuisines while offering a welcoming and vibrant dining atmosphere. Our story began in 2014. "47" is named after its inaugural branch, situated at the intersection of 47th Street and Anawyahtar Road.
